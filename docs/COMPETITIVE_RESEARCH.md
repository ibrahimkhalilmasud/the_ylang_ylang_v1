# COMPETITIVE RESEARCH — Luxury Bali / beachfront villa web patterns

> Per brief §29. Patterns observed across the luxury private-villa and luxury-hospitality
> web category (Elite Havens' own portfolio presentation, and the broader conventions of
> high-end villa/resort sites in the ~USD 1,000+/night tier). Used to inform decisions;
> not copied. Live competitor deep-crawls can be added when the user approves further
> external fetching.

## Patterns worth adopting
| Pattern | Where it's strong | Adopt for Ylang Ylang? |
|---------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Full-bleed cinematic hero, one clear CTA | Nearly all top-tier villa sites | **Yes** — hero photo + "Enquire". |
| Editorial long-form storytelling over feature bullets | Aman, COMO, boutique villa sites | **Yes** — the source prose is excellent; present as spreads. |
| Persistent but quiet booking/enquiry access | Elite Havens, Mr & Mrs Smith | **Yes** — sticky CTA, mobile bottom bar. |
| Rich, generous galleries with lightbox | All | **Yes** — once real assets arrive. |
| Seasonal rate transparency + promotions | Elite Havens/PHV | **Yes** — real table already captured. |
| Room-by-room breakdown with beds/counts | Villa specialists | **Yes** — `/rooms` with per-room detail. |
| "Things to do" / concierge as desire-building content | Resort sites | **Yes** — `/experiences`. |
| Restraint: slow motion, lots of whitespace | Aman, Bulgari | **Yes** — core to "Passionate Serenity". |

## Patterns to reject / avoid
| Pattern | Why reject |
|---------|-----------|
| Autoplay heavy video hero with sound | Performance risk (§22); no approved video yet (D4). |
| Gratuitous WebGL/3D on entry | Slows LCP; no asset justification (D5). |
| Scroll-jacking / full-page snap | Fights usability & booking (§27). |
| Fabricated star ratings / "5-star" badges | Source has no numeric rating (D8, §24). |
| Stock "tropical" imagery as filler | Misrepresents a real, specific property (§9). |
| Aggressive pop-ups / cookie walls | Privacy-first stance; degrade UX. |

## Positioning takeaway
The property's genuine differentiators — **absolute beachfront on black volcanic sand**,
**complete privacy** (deserted beach, tidal-river access, no resort crowds), **authentic
Balinese culture** (Saba's Legong dancers, temple blessings), and **exceptional
personalised service** — are stronger and more credible than generic "luxury" tropes.
The design should lead with these real, verifiable strengths rather than importing a
homogenised luxury look.
