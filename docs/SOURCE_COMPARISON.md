## 1. Bedroom configuration — twin vs "convertible to King"
| Source | Wording |
|--------|---------|
| Quick Facts | "2 twin-bedded suites **convertible to Kings**" |
| Explore the Villa | "Junior Rooms, each furnished with **twin beds** — one with canopied mosquito nets — very suitable for children" |
- **Nature:** Not a contradiction of fact, but a difference of emphasis. Quick Facts
  markets flexibility (couples); Explore frames them as children's rooms.
- **Resolution:** Present Explore's detailed description as the primary copy; mention
  King-convertibility as an available option. Preserve both source wordings in the audit.

## 2. Events capacity — "up to 70" vs "up to 20 at no charge"
| Source | Wording |
|--------|---------|
| Quick Facts | "Weddings and special events for **up to 70 people standing (50 sitting)** can be arranged with prior notice." |
| Weddings | "Dinner parties and gatherings with **up to 20 guests** can be catered for within the villa **at no additional charge**… MAX STANDING 70 PAX / MAX SITTING 50 PAX." |
- **Resolution:** Both are true and complementary — 20 = included threshold, 70/50 =
  arranged maximum. Present together; never state "70 included."
